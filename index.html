ABOUT

Precision science

meets machine learning


I am a tenured Associate Professor at Michigan State University's College of Human Medicine, jointly appointed in the Office of Medical Education Research and Development and the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics. My research identity centers on being a dual-trained psychometrician and biostatistician applying precision medicine methodology to precision medical education.

My methodological work bridges causal inference, individual treatment effects (ITE), latent variable modeling, and deep learning. I design ML-based evaluation systems — integrating NLP, computer vision, and LLM-based annotation — to analyze large-scale, multimodal behavioral data from clinical training environments.

As a Visiting Scholar at Stanford Prevention Research Center, Department of Medicine and UCSF Department of Surgery (2025), I collaborate with interdisciplinary teams on interpretable ML frameworks for wearable data phenotyping, longitudinal health modeling, and AI applications in clinical evaluation. I also consult through Quant Quest, my statistical consulting practice, on causal inference, power analysis, clinical trial design, and program evaluation.

Prior to my academic career, I served in Taiwan's Ministry of Education (Bureau of International Cultural and Educational Relations) and hold degrees from Michigan State University, National Chengchi University (Taipei), and National Kaohsiung Normal University.
