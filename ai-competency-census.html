U.S. Medical School AI Competency Census

Courses, curricula, and certificates that teach artificial intelligence, tracked across every LCME-accredited MD program in the United States and Puerto Rico.

Use with caution. This census was compiled from information that schools publish on their websites, catalogs, and in published articles. It is not based on school self-report. Public pages vary in detail and timeliness, so the data are subject to bias. Schools with less public documentation will appear to offer less, and some entries may be incomplete or out of date.

For authoritative information about a specific program, contact that school’s curriculum or program director directly. Institutions that want to correct or update their own entry can email chisq@msu.edu.

Method, status, and how to contribute

Census frame. All programs on the LCME Directory of Accredited MD Programs in the United States (page updated July 22, 2026), including programs with preliminary or provisional status. View the directory.

Current phase. Programs found in an initial targeted scan were re-checked against primary sources. The full protocol is now being applied state by state. Schools not yet reviewed are marked pending; a pending school may already teach AI.

November 8, 2026: data-source caution and contact for institutional updates added; AAMC AI Competencies Across the Learning Continuum (published October 27, 2026) updated from “in development” to published; all 82 programs coded to its six domains (provisional, single coder); AAMC domain chart and filter added; mobile layout added.

Protocol. Fixed searches of each school's website, catalogs, pathway pages, GME pathways, and the published literature; dual independent coding; and confirmation by each school's curriculum lead.

Correct or add a program. Curriculum leads can confirm, correct, or add their school's entry by emailing chisq@msu.edu. Please include the program name, a public source link if available, and the name and role of the person confirming.

Updates. Quarterly versioned releases with a changelog, plus event-driven updates as national standards change. The AAMC published its AI Competencies Across the Learning Continuum on October 27, 2026; inventoried programs are now coded to its six domains (provisional).

Suggested citation. Chang C. U.S. Medical School AI Competency Census [Data set]. Version 0.9 (draft). 2026. DOI to be assigned at release.
